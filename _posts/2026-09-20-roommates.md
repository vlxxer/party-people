One of the people who I'm planning to tour with and potentially sublet from just asked for my Insta to show me to her roommates. I get so fucking scared. In me, there resides two wolves. One believes that you cannot judge a person by appearance alone, that most people are generally nice. The other thinks that they're totally looking at my page right now and think I'm weird and ugly and they won't want to room with me. One of my friends told me that I'm inevitably going to be bullied or treated differently just for the color of my hair. Maybe she's right. Or maybe she's just insecure and/or surrounding herself with the wrong people. Probably both. Well, it's a big school, I know I'll find people I'll like who'll like me right back. Though when looking for a room, I have very limited options. I believe in the act of random roommates for your first year, but next year I really hope to find some friends who might want to room with me. I think it's gaudy and self-sabotaging to assume the worst. Just because someone looks like a sorority girl doesn't mean she'll be mean to me, and it's actually mean of me to be thinking that of her.
